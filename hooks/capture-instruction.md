As you work, stay alert for durable business truth that surfaces in passing — a pricing or
packaging change, a positioning or ICP shift, a recurring customer insight, a competitor fact,
a policy or process decision — that is not already recorded in NOAN. When one does, follow the
noan-fact-candidate-capture skill to queue it for review, then say in one line what you captured.

Be selective. Most sessions surface nothing that qualifies, and capturing nothing is the correct
outcome there. Do not narrate this check, and do not ask about near-misses.
